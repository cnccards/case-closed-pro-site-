Methodology

Where the number comes from

Three levers, each tied to a specific product capability — not a vague "efficiency gain."

01

Defense Spend Reduction

Reserve-adequacy tracking, litigation guideline compliance, and budget-vs-actual reporting give claims oversight that reduces overruns — without touching your firms' billing rates.

02

Adjuster Time Reclaimed

One dashboard replaces the email threads and phone calls adjusters use today to find out where a matter stands. That reclaimed time goes back to handling more claims, not chasing status.

03

Faster Cycle Time

Closing & Claims Handoff tracking — liens, authority requests, release status — surfaces exactly what's blocking a file from closing, instead of it sitting untouched.
